# Part 1: The Night Before

ELLIE: My name is Ellie Hartley. I'm fourteen, and I live on a dairy farm in the Yorkshire Dales, in the north of England.

# The Collar
[bgm mystery.2]
ELLIE: Now, there's one more thing you need to know before I tell you about the night, and it's the collar.
ELLIE: In the spring, I persuaded Dad to try something new. Smart collars. A company lent us twenty of them for free, to test.
ELLIE: They're a bit like a fitness tracker, but for cows. There's a [[sensor]] in the collar that measures how much the cow moves, how long she lies down, and how much she chews.
ELLIE: Chewing is really important. Cows bring their food back up from their stomach and chew it again. It's called chewing the cud. It sounds disgusting, but if a cow suddenly stops chewing, it's often the first sign that she's ill.
ELLIE: And Duchess's collar was the newest kind. It had GPS. So I could see exactly where she was, on a map, on my phone.
ELLIE: Dad thought it was a waste of time. He said, "I know where my cows are, Ellie. They're in the field. Where they've been for two hundred years."
ELLIE: But I loved it. Every night, before I went to sleep, I checked the app. It was a bit like saying goodnight.
ELLIE: On Tuesday night, at ten o'clock, the app said Duchess was in the big barn, lying down, chewing happily.
ELLIE: Outside, it started to rain. [[Heavy rain]]. Then the thunder came.
[sfx thunder]
ELLIE: I fell asleep listening to the rain on the roof.
ELLIE: I didn't hear anything else. And that's the strange part, because our dog, Bess, barks at everything. She barks at the postman. She barks at the wind. Once, she barked at a carrot for twenty minutes.
ELLIE: But that night, Bess didn't bark at all.
