# The Migration
[bgm orchestral.36]
NARRATOR: Eight fifteen a.m. The first members of the [[species]] begin to arrive.
NARRATOR: Here, at the great revolving door, we see a young male. He is new to this environment. His shoes are too shiny. His backpack is too large. He has not yet learned the ways of the office.
NARRATOR: He approaches the security gate, and searches for his pass. It is not in his left pocket. It is not in his right pocket. A queue begins to form behind him. The other [[commuters|commuter]] watch in silence. Nobody helps. In this habitat, it is every human for himself.
TYLER (nervous): Sorry. Sorry. It's in here somewhere. Sorry.
NARRATOR: The young male will apologise eleven times before he finds the pass. This is normal behaviour for the species, especially in Britain, where the apology is used not only to say sorry, but also as a greeting, a warning, and a way of filling silence.
NARRATOR: Next, the group must enter the lift.
NARRATOR: The lift is one of the most dangerous environments in the office. Up to twelve strangers are forced into a small metal box, closer together than they would ever choose to be. To survive, they have developed a remarkable strategy.
NARRATOR (whisper): They pretend that nobody else exists.
NARRATOR: Every member of the group stares at the numbers above the door, as if they contain the secrets of the universe. Eye contact is avoided at all costs. Speaking is extremely rare. If two humans who know each other enter the lift together, they will begin a conversation, and then immediately regret it, because everybody can hear.
GARY: So, good weekend?
MEI: Yes, thanks. You?
GARY: Yeah, good. Busy.
MEI: Busy is good.
GARY: Yeah.
NARRATOR: The rest of the journey, three floors, passes in complete silence. It is one of the longest silences in the natural world.

# The Meeting

# The Battle for the Window
[bgm sports.41 0.13]
NARRATOR: Eleven fifteen. And here, we witness something truly remarkable. Something very few people outside the office have ever seen.
NARRATOR: Until recently, every worker in this habitat had a desk of their own. A territory. They decorated it with photographs of their families, small plants, and mugs with jokes on them. These territories were respected. A desk was a home.
NARRATOR: But then came a terrible event. The company [[introduced|introduce]] "hot-desking". There are no longer enough desks for everybody, and nobody owns a desk at all. Each morning, it is first come, first served.
NARRATOR: The result has been a fierce [[competition]], especially for the most valuable territory in the entire office. The desk by the window.
NARRATOR: It has natural light. It has a view of the car park, and a small tree. It is as far as possible from Gary. And it is next to the radiator.
NARRATOR: Watch. A female has left the window desk to go to the toilet. She has left behind her jacket on the chair, a clear signal that the territory is taken.
NARRATOR: But Tyler, the young male, has been watching. He approaches. He looks left. He looks right. He moves the jacket, very gently, to a different chair. And he sits down.
NARRATOR (dramatic): This is a serious mistake.
